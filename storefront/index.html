Who made this

Every saree starts with a needle, not a screen.

We began with embroidered tops and kept asking the same question: who stitched this, and how long did it take? So we now work directly with small workshops and name each technique on every piece.

A single border of chain stitch is around eleven hours at the frame.

Our commitments

  • Hand-worked, and named

    Each listing says which stitch or print was used and whether it was done by hand or on a frame.

  • Small workshops, direct

    We pay workshops a fixed piece rate agreed before the order, not after.

  • Real fibres only

    Cotton, linen and silk-cotton. No screen-printed synthetics, no blends we can't name.
